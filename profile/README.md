## Hi there 👋

The Vision Analysis and Security (**VAS**) lab is a research group within the Institute of Artificial Intelligence at Ocean University of China, directed by Dr. [Yuezun Li](https://yuezunli.github.io/). Our lab focuses on multimedia forensics, computer vision and vision security, with a focus on cutting-edge exploration and innovation in these areas. Recently, we have particularly concentrated on face forensics and have achieved significant progress. Our research has been published in top international conferences and journals such as NeurIPS, ICCV, CVPR, ECCV, TIFS, and TCSVT.

📢 **Openings available!** We invite students who are passionate about scientific research and cutting-edge technologies, enjoy mathematics, and have strong programming skills to join our group. Please contact us at **liyuezun AT ouc.edu.cn**.
