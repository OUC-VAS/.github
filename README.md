# OUC-VAS

Vision Analysis and Security (VAS) lab is a researh group of the Institute of Artificial Intelligence at Ocean University of China, directed by Dr. [Yuezun Li](https://yuezunli.github.io/). Our lab focuses on multimedia forensics, computer vision and vision security, with a focus on cutting-edge exploration and innovation in these areas. Recently, our lab particularly concentrates on face forensics and has made important progress. The reserach outcomes have been published in top international conferences and journals such as NeurIPS, ICCV, CVPR, ECCV, TIFS, and TCSVT.

📢 Opening! We welcome students who are interested in scientific research and cutting-edge technologies, have a passion for mathematics, and are skilled in programming to join our research group. You can contact us at liyuezun AT ouc.edu.cn.
